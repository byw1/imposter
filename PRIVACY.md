# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 11 October 2026_

Imposter is a party game that works entirely on your phone. This policy is
short because there is very little to say.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** Imposter has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking and no servers. It never sends anything about you or your games
anywhere. The app does not use the internet at all, and its code includes a
test that fails the build if anything tries to.

## What stays on your phone

The names of the people you play with, your house rules, the topics you make,
the game in progress and the stats from past rounds are stored on your device
only. Nobody else, including us, can see them. Deleting the app deletes them,
and Settings has a button to clear the stats.

## Permissions

None. Imposter doesn't ask for the camera, microphone, photos, contacts,
location or notifications.

## Third parties

Imposter contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and
shares no data with anyone, because it has none to share. Apple handles App
Store downloads under its own privacy policy.

## Children

Imposter collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this ever changes, this page will say so before the app does.
